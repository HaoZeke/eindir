* Code of Conduct

The =eindir= project adopts the [[https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/][Contributor Covenant]] (v2.1).

* Our Pledge

Project spaces should remain open, respectful, and focused on the work.
Participation is welcome across backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and technical domains.

* Standards

Helpful participation includes reproducible reports, concrete examples, constructive review, and patience with users and maintainers.
Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, personal attacks, publication of private information, or sustained disruption of technical discussion.

* Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, moderate discussions, or restrict participation when conduct makes project work harder for other contributors.
