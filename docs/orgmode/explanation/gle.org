* The problem it solves

Langevin dynamics samples the Boltzmann distribution by adding friction and noise to Hamiltonian dynamics.
With white noise there is one friction constant =gamma=, and a harmonic mode of frequency =omega= decorrelates fastest near critical damping, when =gamma= is of order =omega=.
An ill-conditioned objective has curvatures, and so frequencies, spread over orders of magnitude, and one =gamma= leaves most of them over- or under-damped.

The generalized Langevin equation (GLE) replaces the single friction by a memory kernel.
Ceriotti, Bussi and Parrinello showed that a kernel built from a few auxiliary momenta, coupled linearly to the physical momentum, gives a Markovian system that can be integrated exactly and tuned so that the friction, and with it the sampling efficiency, is nearly flat over a chosen band of frequencies [1, 2].

* What eindir computes

For each coordinate, the state is a vector =s= of =ns + 1= entries: the mass-scaled physical momentum in =s[0]= and =ns= auxiliary momenta.
Its dynamics is the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process =ds = -A s dt + B dW=, with drift matrix =A= and diffusion matrix =B=.
The exact solution over one step =dt= is

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
s <- T s + S xi,   T = exp(-dt A),   S S^T = kb (C - T C T^T),
#+END_EXAMPLE

with =xi= standard normal and =C= the stationary covariance.
=GleThermostat::new= computes =T= with =matrix_exp= (Taylor series with scaling and squaring) and =S= with =ldl_sqrt=, a pivoted LDL^T square root that tolerates the nearly singular matrix that small =dt= produces.
These follow the =ThermoGLE= propagator of i-PI [3].
Neither needs a linear-algebra library, because the matrices have only =ns + 1= rows.

* Correctness from C, speed from A

The stationary distribution of the process is =N(0, kb C)=, whatever the drift.
Setting =C = temperature * I=, as =GleThermostat::canonical= does, makes the physical momentum Boltzmann-distributed for any valid =A=.
The drift only decides how fast each frequency decorrelates.
This is the decoupling of correctness from efficiency that Ceriotti, Bussi and Parrinello call colored-noise thermostats "à la carte" [2], and it is why one drift matrix can be reused at every temperature.

* The optimal-sampling drift

=optimal_sampling_drift(omega0)= returns a fitted =A= with six damped-oscillator baths, each a pair of auxiliary momenta with eigenvalues =-Gamma ± i Omega=, skew-coupled to the physical momentum.
Spreading the bath frequencies over the band =[omega0, 100 omega0]= flattens the friction across it.
The parameters were fitted to maximise the worst normalised sampling efficiency over the band, following the procedure of the GLE input generator at [[https://gle4md.org][gle4md.org]]; the fit lives in =experiments/colored_noise_sampling.py= of anneal.
Since =A= has units of frequency, scaling the band only multiplies the matrix by =omega0=.

The symmetric part =A + A^T= of this matrix is diagonal and non-negative, so with =C = I= the fluctuation-dissipation relation =A C + C A^T = B B^T= has a real solution =B=, and the canonical thermostat is valid.

* Who uses it

The GLE Langevin driver of [[https://github.com/HaoZeke/anneal][anneal]] builds the thermostat once, draws the initial auxiliary state with =sample_stationary=, and calls =step= between force kicks.
The objective values and the accept decisions stay in anneal.
