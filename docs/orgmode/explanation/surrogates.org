* Surrogate Mechanics

The additive surrogate is a rank-1 functional tensor train.
Its tempered density factorises, enabling O(d) independence sampling.
The Metropolis correction on the true objective removes the mean-field bias while preserving the "one fitted object, multiple slots" property.
